## Hi there! 👋 I'm Joseph Anya

I'm a product engineer and founder building **digital health infrastructure for Africa**.

At **[Lifebox Labs](https://lifebox.ng)**, I'm developing a portfolio of consumer health brands — starting with **Priv Health** (men's digital health) and **Custodia Health** (chronic disease management).

---

### What I work on

Most of my engineering time sits at the intersection of **AI systems and clinical infrastructure**:

- **RAG pipelines for clinical reasoning** — synthesizing patient history, labs, biomarkers, and care plans into real-time guidance
- **On-device AI inference** — offline-first mobile apps for community health workers in low-connectivity settings (Llama.cpp + Flutter)
- **Health data interoperability** — HL7/FHIR integrations, HIPAA-compliant architecture, EHR bidirectional sync
- **MCP tooling** — building open protocol servers for financial and health data APIs
